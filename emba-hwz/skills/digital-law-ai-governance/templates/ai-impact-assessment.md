## K. Residual risk

After mitigations, what risks remain? Rate and justify.

- **High** — risks of harm to individuals or society that remain after all reasonable mitigations.
- **Medium** — manageable risks with monitoring.
- **Low** — acceptable for the use case.

If residual risk is **high**, the use case should not be deployed without additional senior approval and documented acceptance.
