# Integrity Program Design

Use to assess and design the organizational infrastructure that supports ethical behavior and resists the conditions that produce ethical failure. Distinct from a compliance program, though it overlaps.

## 6. Education

**Current ethics training:**
> _Annual generic? Scenario-based? Role-specific?_

**What works in good integrity education:**
- Real-situation scenarios from this company's industry
- Active discussion, not passive video
- Tailored to role (executive ethics differs from frontline)
- Refresh on emerging risks (AI ethics, geopolitical, anti-trust, etc.)
- Sufficient frequency to remain present

## 8. Compensation and selection

**Compensation alignment:**
- Are bonuses subject to clawback for ethical violations?
- Is integrity considered in promotion decisions?
- Are aggressive numerical targets paired with ethical guardrails?

**Selection:**
- Are integrity considerations in hiring criteria, especially for senior roles?
- Are reference checks rigorous?
- Is the onboarding integrity training credible?
