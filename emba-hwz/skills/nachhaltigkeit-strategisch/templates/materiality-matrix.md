## 4. Materiality matrix

_Plot the scored topics. The upper right quadrant (high on both) is clearly material. Upper-left and lower-right (high on one) need judgment under CSRD double-materiality._

```
Impact materiality
       5 |     M       M
       4 |   M   M       M
       3 |     S   S
       2 |   x   x
       1 | x   x
         +-----------------> Financial materiality
           1  2  3  4  5
```

_M = material, S = secondary, x = not material_
