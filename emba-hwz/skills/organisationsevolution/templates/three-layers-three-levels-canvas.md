## The integration test

**For each cell currently being worked on:**

- How does it connect to the adjacent cells?
- Is the work coherent across cells, or are there contradictions?
- Where is the friction between cells that is slowing the evolution?

Example: "Org systems" work introducing OKRs, while "team culture" rewards individual heroics — predictable failure.
