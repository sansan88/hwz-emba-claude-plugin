## 4. The seven filter questions

### Q1 — Transparency
Is the measurement transparent to the people being measured? Do they know what is collected, how often, and for what purpose?

- [ ] Yes — documented in [where]
- [ ] Partial — gaps in: [...]
- [ ] No

### Q2 — Voice
Do the people being measured (or their representatives) have meaningful voice in how the data is used?

- [ ] Yes — through [mechanism]
- [ ] Advisory only
- [ ] No

### Q3 — Aggregation
Is the data used at the individual level, or only aggregated to team/org?

- [ ] Individual — justification: ...
- [ ] Team-level only
- [ ] Org-level only

### Q4 — Symmetry
Are leaders measured by the same instruments, with the same granularity, as the workers below them?

- [ ] Yes
- [ ] Partly
- [ ] No

### Q5 — Purpose limitation
Is the data ringfenced to its named purpose? Could it be repurposed silently?

- [ ] Strict purpose limitation, with sunset clause
- [ ] Some flexibility, controls in place
- [ ] Open-ended

### Q6 — Worst-case use
If management intent shifted in a year, what is the maximum harmful use of this data?

>

### Q7 — Reversibility
Can the measurement be stopped easily if it produces harm? Is the data deletable?

- [ ] Yes — and tested
- [ ] In principle
- [ ] No
