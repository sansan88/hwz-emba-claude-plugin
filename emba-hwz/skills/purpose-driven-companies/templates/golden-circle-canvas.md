## Why (innermost)

**The belief, the cause, the deeper commitment:**

>

### Tests

- [ ] **Distinctive:** Would a competitor's Why read differently?
- [ ] **About contribution:** Is it about making a difference, not about commercial success?
- [ ] **Stable:** Will it still be true in 20 years?
- [ ] **Specific:** Not "delight customers" generic?

## The inside-out narrative

**The integrated narrative:**

> _"In everything we do, we believe in [Why]. We pursue this by [How 1, How 2, How 3]. We just happen to [What 1, What 2, What 3]."_

### Tests

- [ ] **Reads naturally:** Sounds like us, not like a template
- [ ] **Behavior-supported:** Each layer is supported by actual operating behavior
- [ ] **Memorable:** A senior leader could deliver this in 90 seconds without notes

## Stress-tests across the whole

### The competitor swap
Replace our company name with a competitor's. Does the Golden Circle still read true?

If yes → the Golden Circle is generic; sharpen the differentiators.

### The recent decisions test

### The recruitment test
Would the Golden Circle attract the kind of employees who would amplify it? Or would it attract generic talent who would hollow it out?

>

### The "and we just happen to" test
Complete the sentence: "We believe in X, so we approach work in Y way, and we just happen to..."

> _If "just happen to" feels right, the model is coherent. If it feels forced, iterate._
