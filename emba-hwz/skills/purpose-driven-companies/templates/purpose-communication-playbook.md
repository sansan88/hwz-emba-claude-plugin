## 3. External communication

### Master narrative
The story we tell across all external touchpoints. Consistent in core elements; varied in surface for different audiences.

>

### Audience adaptations

**For customers:**
> _Emphasize what the purpose means for them — what they get, what they connect with by choosing us._

**For investors:**
> _Emphasize how purpose produces business outcomes — customer loyalty, talent, innovation, risk._

**For employees and candidates:**
> _Emphasize what work here means, what the company contributes, why their daily work matters._

**For partners and suppliers:**
> _Emphasize how we work together, what we hold as standards._

**For regulators and policymakers:**
> _Emphasize the contribution we make to the public interest, the standards we hold._

**For media:**
> _Emphasize the substantive story — signature programs, real outcomes, honest stance on hard topics._

## 5. Campaign architecture

How marketing investment ties to purpose:

**Always-on:**
> _The sustained baseline of communication that keeps the purpose visible. Signature programs are the spine._

**Tentpole campaigns:**
> _Annual or twice-yearly larger campaigns that dramatize specific aspects of the purpose._

**Reactive:**
> _Capacity to respond to current events when relevant — but only with credibility built up over time. Bandwagon-jumping fails._

## 6. Crisis playbook

When something goes wrong (operational, regulatory, social), how does the purpose guide response?

- **First instinct:** "What does our purpose require us to do here?" before "what does PR say?"
- **Honest acknowledgment:** purpose is not the get-out-of-jail card; admit what is true
- **Action over statement:** what changes operationally because of this event?
- **Stakeholder engagement:** the relationships built through purpose-aligned behavior are the asset that gets us through

## 7. Anti-greenwashing discipline

- [ ] All claims substantiable
- [ ] Methodology documented for impact claims
- [ ] No selective spotlighting that hides counterevidence
- [ ] Clear distinction between aspiration and accomplishment
- [ ] Public-facing disclosures aligned with sustainability reporting (CSRD / IFRS S2)
- [ ] Legal review for claims with regulatory exposure

## 8. The senior leader's purpose practice

**Each senior leader commits to:**

- Telling the purpose story in their own words (not corporate boilerplate)
- Connecting day-to-day decisions to the purpose when speaking with their teams
- Naming when they catch themselves making decisions against the purpose
- Bringing the purpose lens into operational meetings, not just communications meetings
