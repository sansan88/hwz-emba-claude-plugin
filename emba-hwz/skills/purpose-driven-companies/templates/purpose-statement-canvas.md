## 9. The first draft purpose statement

> _"We exist to [contribution] for [beneficiary], by [distinctive how]."_

## 10. The stress tests

- [ ] **Competitor test** — would the statement read true with a competitor's name in place of ours? If yes, sharpen.
- [ ] **Trade-off test** — does this statement imply trade-offs we are willing to make?
- [ ] **Decision test** — could it guide a real decision in front of us this month?
- [ ] **Specificity test** — is it specific to this company?
- [ ] **Sustained test** — will it still be true in 10 years?
- [ ] **Recognition test** — do our employees and customers recognize us in it?

## 12. The operational commitments

For the purpose to be real, the senior team commits to:

- **Trade-offs:** _e.g., we will pass on customer segments that we cannot serve with the depth our purpose requires_
- **KPIs:** _e.g., 20% of executive comp tied to purpose-aligned metrics_
- **Hiring:** _e.g., purpose-fit included in senior-role rubrics_
- **Capital:** _e.g., 15% of capex envelope reserved for purpose-aligned innovation_
