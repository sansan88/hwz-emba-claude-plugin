## 7. The short version

## 8. The narrative

_1–2 pages drafted for internal use. Walks through the contribution, the beneficiaries, the trade-offs, the standards. Not marketing copy._

>

## 9. The four tests

- [ ] **Recognition** — Do frontline employees recognize this as the company they work for?
- [ ] **Trade-off** — Does this imply trade-offs we are actually willing to make?
- [ ] **Awkward decision** — Would this guide a real ambiguous decision in front of us?
- [ ] **Disappearance** — Is the answer to "what would be lost" something not also true of competitors?
