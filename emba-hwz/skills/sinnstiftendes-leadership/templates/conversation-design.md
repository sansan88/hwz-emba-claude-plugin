## 6. What I will listen for

_Beyond the content._

- The emotion underneath the words
- What is not being said
- Where values are being touched
- What they may be testing in me
- The pace and breath of the conversation

## 8. What I will say if it gets hard

_Prepare a few honest phrases for the moments you might otherwise reach for defensiveness._

- "Let me sit with what you just said for a moment."
- "I notice this is difficult. I want to make sure I'm hearing you."
- "I have a reaction to that. Let me name it rather than act it."
- "I don't have a clean answer to that. Can we explore it?"

## 11. Self-care after

_Hard conversations cost the leader too. Don't bypass this._

- 5 minutes alone after, before the next meeting
- Brief reflection: what served, what didn't
- If it was particularly hard, longer reflection later in the day
