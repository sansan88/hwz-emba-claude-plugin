## Applying this to someone I lead

_For each direct report, ask honestly:_

- Do I know their core values? Not their needs — their values.
- Have I asked? In what way?
- What current work decision could honor their values better?
- Where am I asking them to violate their values, and is that necessary?

## Practice schedule

- **Quarterly:** revisit this canvas; values may shift over time
- **Annually:** a deeper reflection, possibly in retreat or coaching
- **In transition moments:** when starting a new role, ending a major project, facing a hard decision

## The honest line for myself

_If I look at my values and my actual work life, what is the most uncomfortable truth?_

>

Don't bypass this. It is often the most important data.
