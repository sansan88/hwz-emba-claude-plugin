# Ethical Blindness Check

Use to audit a current decision, situation, or organizational pattern for the conditions that produce ethical blindness. Honest answers required; the value of the check is in the discomfort it surfaces.

---

## 1. The situation

> _The decision, practice, or pattern being examined. Specific enough to engage._

## 2. The mechanism diagnostic

### Self-deception
**How is this situation being described internally?**
>

**What would a trusted outsider, briefed fully, call what is actually happening here?**
>

**Where is the gap between the two descriptions?**
>

### Routine and gradualism
**How long has this practice been going on?**
>

**How does the current state compare to where the practice started?**
>

**If we showed today's practice to ourselves three years ago, what would we think?**
>

### Strong frames
**What frames dominate the discussion of this situation?**
- [ ] Financial
- [ ] Technical
- [ ] Legal / compliance
- [ ] Competitive
- [ ] Political
- [ ] Other:

**What dimensions are excluded by these frames?**
>

**If we forced an ethical frame into the conversation, what would change?**
>

### In-group dynamics
**Who is in the in-group on this decision?**
>

**Whose voice is missing or marginalized?**
>

**What does the in-group lose by acknowledging the concern?**
>

**Who recently left or was sidelined who would have raised concerns?**
>

### Anonymous others
**Who is affected by this decision?**
>

**Have they been named specifically and imagined concretely?**
>

**If we had to talk to one of them tomorrow, what would we say?**
>
