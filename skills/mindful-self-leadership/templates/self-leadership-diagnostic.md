## 8. Score interpretation

**Total score:** _Add up all numeric responses._

- **120+** — Significant overload. Personal practice + structural change both needed urgently.
- **80–120** — Common for senior leaders. Steady practice will produce visible difference in 3–6 months.
- **50–80** — Reasonable baseline. Practice will deepen and sustain.
- **<50** — Strong foundation; the work is depth not basics.
