## 6. Aaker tests

- [ ] **Stick test:** Committed for at least 3 years, with credible 3 more years funded
- [ ] **Name test:** Has its own name and identity
- [ ] **Connection test:** Visibly connects to purpose and business
- [ ] **Impact test:** Will produce real outcomes that can be evidenced
- [ ] **Authenticity test:** Could survive journalist substantiation
